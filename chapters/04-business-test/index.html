01 · PERFORMANCE

Flexibility does not automatically translate into better predictive performance.

Greater flexibility does not automatically translate into better predictive performance.

We therefore start with deviance: a likelihood-based measure of how well the model describes the observed claim experience.

MATHEMATICAL FRAMEWORK
\[D_{\mathrm{P}} = 2\sum_i w_i\left[y_i\log\left(\frac{y_i}{\hat y_i}\right)-\left(y_i-\hat y_i\right)\right]\]

The smaller the Poisson deviance, the better the model describes the observed claim counts under the Poisson likelihood, taking exposure into account.

\[D_{\Gamma} = 2\sum_i w_i\left[\log\left(\frac{\hat y_i}{y_i}\right)+\frac{y_i}{\hat y_i}-1\right]\]

The analogous Gamma deviance measures how well the model describes observed claim severities.

For frequency, \(w_i\) reflects exposure; for severity, \(w_i\) reflects the number of claims represented by the observation.

02 · DISCRIMINATION

Can the model really identify risks?

When it comes to pricing, it's not enough to get it right on average.

We want to identify contracts with higher expected claim risk as early as possible in the ranking.

—Highest frequency Gini on the test set.
MATHEMATICAL FRAMEWORK
\[G = 1 - 2\int_0^1 L(p)\,\mathrm{d}p\]

The higher the Gini coefficient, the more clearly the model distinguishes between contracts with lower and higher risk.

\[\operatorname{Lift}_{10\%} = \frac{\mathrm{Risk}_{\mathrm{top\ 10\%}}}{\mathrm{Risk}_{\mathrm{portfolio}}}\]

A lift of 1.00 is the portfolio average. The current audited export does not contain the point-level lift data needed to report a Top-10% value.

03 · DECISION

Complexity comes at a price.

GLM is easier to read. ML is more flexible. Both statements can be true at the same time.

Model selection must therefore take into account statistical quality, calibration, interpretability, and operational overhead collectively.

NOT „Which model is the most complex?“
RATHER „What additional complexity improves decision-making and based on which metrics?“
DECISION-MAKING LOGIC
\[\mathrm{Benefits} \approx \mathrm{better\ Risk Classification}+\mathrm{better\ Calibration}\]
\[\mathrm{Costs} \approx \mathrm{Complexity}+\mathrm{ExplanatoryEffort}+\mathrm{Operation}\]
